Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Contact:

NUMERS stores spreadsheets, programmes and settings on the device only (iPhone, iPad or Mac). No account, no ads, no data selling.

Data processed

  • Days, spreadsheets, photo numbers and assigned numbers you enter
  • Imported contest programme (PDF or Excel) and photo folder paths, on Mac
  • Device name used for local sync

Local network

iPhone ↔ Mac sync uses local Wi‑Fi and, if needed, Bluetooth. Data stays between your devices. It does not go through a Picaza server.
