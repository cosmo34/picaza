Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Contact: contact@picaza.fr

PAPAYA is published by Picaza. The app is for workouts, meals and a daily agenda. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a health professional.

Data

The account, profile (name, email, height, weight, age, goal), sessions, meals, shopping list, profile photo and workout videos stay on the device. The password is stored as a hash. Step counts come from the phone.

PAPAYA does not sell this data, does not show third-party ads, and does not send the account to a Picaza server.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: record a session, only if you allow it.
  • Photos: choose a profile photo.
  • Motion: read today’s step count.

Deletion

In Profile, “Supprimer le compte” deletes the account and related data from that device. You can also revoke permissions in system settings.

Contact

Questions about this data: contact@picaza.fr.
