Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Contact:

NUMERS for iPhone / iPad is on the App Store. The Mac app can still be downloaded from the product page.

Install on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open NUMERS on the App Store or search for NUMERS.
  2. Install the app, then create a day and a spreadsheet.
  3. Enter the photo number and the assigned rider number. No account is required.

Install on Mac

Download the archive from the product page, unzip, move NUMERS to Applications. On first launch, allow the app in Settings → Privacy & Security if macOS blocks it.

Sync

iPhone and Mac must be on, nearby, with Wi‑Fi (or Bluetooth) enabled. Spreadsheets flow from iPhone to Mac.
